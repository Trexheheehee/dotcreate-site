# v4 references: what we borrowed

Studied 15 Nov 2026 with headless Chrome (screens in `_ref/`), plus computed styles from the live DOM.

## bitmapstudio.com
- Type: Funnel Sans / Funnel Display, weight 400 for headlines, 64 to 80px with about -2% tracking, body 16px/24px. No uppercase shouting, no outlines.
- Stats: two huge numbers ("10M+", "90+") with a small plain label underneath and a thin vertical divider between them.
- Services as a numbered list (01 to 04) separated by hairlines, each with a short paragraph and a small arrow.
- Restraint: one neutral surface per section, black pill primary button and ghost secondary, generous whitespace.
- Footer: short brand line on the left, 4 tidy link columns on the right, small base row.

**Borrowed:** weight 500 (not black) display type with tight negative tracking; the numbered hairline services list; the two-button pair (solid + ghost); calm footer columns.

## shownmedia.com
- Type: Roslindale Display Condensed for the hero (88px, -3% tracking), with the second line in a single accent colour; Inter Display and Manrope for UI.
- Stats bar: "63M+ Views Generated / $10M+ / 1963+ Videos" in one row between two full-width hairlines, with small "+" cross marks at the line ends. Numbers large, label small and beside them.
- Floating dark pill nav with a white "Book a call" pill inside it.
- Giant chrome wordmark at the very bottom of the footer, cropped by the page edge.
- 3D object in the hero, lit with one saturated colour on black.

**Borrowed:** the hairline stats bar with "+" ticks and labels beside the numbers; the floating blurred pill nav with a solid pill CTA; the oversized cropped footer wordmark (toned down to a dark gradient); the second headline line in a different tone (we use grey instead of a colour); a single lit 3D object as the hero subject.

## skale.solutions
- Type: Inter Display 42px/500 with -4% tracking, Roboto Mono 12px for tiny corner meta (domain, local time).
- Layout framed by thin vertical guide lines, very few elements, lots of air.
- Client logos as a monochrome grid, all at the same optical size and grey.
- The one accent colour (red) is used only in the logo and one word.

**Borrowed:** monochrome client grid with hairline cells (real SVGs only for Grok, Lightspeed, Whop, the rest as set type); mono used only for a couple of meta labels (the "Fig. 01" caption, section indexes); local time in the footer; accent colour limited to the primary button, the status dot and the 3D rim light.

## Deliberately dropped from v3
Ticker overload, giant stroked ghost type, the diagonal split section, heavy uppercase everywhere, and dozens of tiny mono labels.
